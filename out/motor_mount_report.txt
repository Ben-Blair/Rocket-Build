MOTOR MOUNT -- the last unsized structure in the vehicle
Model: design/motor_mount.py   Verdict carried by: scripts/baseline.py
Motor: J449BS  dia 54.0 x 321.0 mm, 1.122 kg loaded / 0.498 kg dry

============================================================================================
WHY THIS WAS NEVER SIZED, AND WHAT IT WAS HIDING
============================================================================================
  design/mass.py:70 carries motor_mount_centering_rings = 0.250 kg and :229 puts it
  at the motor's own mid-station. That is the whole of the design. scripts/make_ork.py
  needed SOMETHING to hand OpenRocket and typed 54 mm ID / 1.5 mm wall / 331 mm long
  into itself; docs/04 says 416 mm for the same part. Neither number is imported by
  anything in design/, and the two have never been reconciled.

  An allowance nobody turned into a part, for the fifth time -- after correction 20's
  bulkhead, correction 33's harness, correction 38's access plates and correction 41's
  sled. The pattern is now reliable enough to be a search strategy.

============================================================================================
THE FIRST FINDING -- the frozen 12 mm fin tab and a 54 mm mount tube cannot coexist
============================================================================================
  scripts/make_cad_profiles.py's TAB_DEPTH = 12.00 mm is measured inward from the
  booster tube's OUTER radius, so the tab tip sits at R 27.70 mm.

    booster OD                          R    39.70 mm
    booster ID                          R    37.40 mm
    tab tip at the frozen 12.00 mm      R    27.70 mm
    mount tube OD (this file)           R    28.55 mm   <- the tab was 0.85 mm INSIDE it
    mount tube ID                       R    27.25 mm
    bare motor case                     R    27.00 mm   <- 0.70 mm clear: what 12.00 was really drawn against

  So the 12 mm tab describes a fin bonded to the BARE MOTOR CASE, while design/flutter.py
  and scripts/baseline.py both quote the aft-fin flutter margin for a tab 'bonded
  through the wall to the MOTOR MOUNT'. Four AftFin bodies already occupy R 27.70 in
  the Fusion document. This was never going to survive an interference check; it
  survived because there was nothing yet to check it against.

  DERIVED, NOT CHOSEN: tab depth = booster OR - mount tube OR = 11.15 mm.
  The tab then stands 8.85 mm proud of the airframe bore and lands tangent on the mount tube,
  giving 1777 mm2 of tab-to-tube bond across 4 fins with no fillet credited.

============================================================================================
THE SECOND FINDING -- the forward bulkhead and the thrust face are the same part
============================================================================================
  booster tube                          416.28 mm
  motor, aft-flush                      321.00 mm
  forward margin                         95.28 mm
    less coupler engagement          -   79.40 mm
    less joints.BULKHEAD_ALLOWANCE   -   12.00 mm
    = what the BUDGET leaves              3.88 mm

  There is no room for a separate thrust plate and there does not need to be. One
  disc closes the drogue compartment on its forward face, anchors the drogue harness's
  aft U-bolt there, and presents its aft face to the motor. That is exactly why
  design/seal.py handed this part to the motor mount rather than sizing it as a third
  bulkhead -- a reason seal.py stated without knowing.

  The disc as sized is 4.8 mm, not the 12.0 mm the allowance charges, so the real clear
  gap is 11.08 mm. That is not slack to spend.

============================================================================================
THE THIRD FINDING -- that gap is a closed volume in front of a live ejection charge
============================================================================================
  The Cesaroni Pro54 ships with an ejection charge in its forward closure. This
  vehicle deploys on an independent altimeter, and nothing in this project has ever
  said what happens to the motor's own charge.

  volume bounded by mount tube bore, forward closure and this disc:  25.85 cm3
  disc capacity (seal.py's own expression):                          6.69 MPa

    charge      pressure      over capacity
     0.8 g       6.78 MPa      1.01x
     1.0 g       8.48 MPa      1.27x
     1.2 g      10.17 MPa      1.52x
     1.4 g      11.87 MPa      1.77x
     1.6 g      13.56 MPa      2.03x

  It fails at every plausible charge mass, so the conclusion does not turn on the
  assumed figure -- which is just as well, because Cesaroni does not publish one.
  DESIGN ANSWER: a PLUGGED forward closure (a purchase, not a modification), recorded
  as motor_mount.FORWARD_CLOSURE_PLUGGED = True. Venting the gap is the
  alternative and it is worse: a hole through a pressure boundary to solve what a
  different part number solves for nothing.

============================================================================================
WHAT CARRIES WHAT -- and the headline is that the rings do not carry thrust
============================================================================================
  peak thrust                               586.9 N
  thrust into the forward bulkhead           6.60 MPa       73x   annular land 10.15 mm
  drogue stuck-joint pressure              121.82 MPa      3.9x   at 1699 kPa
  drogue U-bolt, bare 6 mm footprint        96.73 MPa      5.0x   at 1504 N
  ring glue lines, REDUNDANT path            0.51 MPa       12x   293 N each
  retainer base bond                         0.05 MPa      115x   at 147 N
  mount tube, full thrust (redundant)        2.58 MPa      186x   buckling 39x

  THRUST ENTERS THE AIRFRAME AT THE FORWARD BULKHEAD, not through the rings. The
  motor's forward closure bears on that disc through the mount tube's bore, and the
  load travels forward into the vehicle it is pushing. The rings align the motor, tie
  the fin tabs in, and carry the motor's mass laterally. They are checked against the
  full thrust anyway, as the redundant path -- a load path with one member is not one.

  Neither the tube wall nor the ring thickness is set by stress:
    mount tube wall   1.30 mm -- the practical minimum wall -- two rings and a retainer base bond to this tube's outside; local buckling alone would have cleared 2x at 0.9 mm
    ring thickness    3.20 mm -- the practical minimum gauge -- a thinner ring tips in its own fillet and cannot be bonded square; the glue line alone would have cleared 2x at 1.6 mm
  Same shape of result as design/access_bulkhead.py's two plates and design/venting.py's
  port sizing: the model gives the floor, practice gives the design point.

============================================================================================
WHAT IS STILL OPEN
============================================================================================
  * THE FIN ROOT MOMENT. The tab-to-mount-tube bond is checked here for AREA and for
    CLEARANCE. The moment it carries has never been computed by anything in this
    project, and design/flutter.py says the same about its own ideal-rigid-root
    assumption. Swinging the finished fin is what settles it.
  * THE MOUNT TUBE'S MATERIAL. Every margin above uses G-10 SHEET properties for a
    filament-wound tube, because design/materials.py has nothing else. The margins
    that matter are all well clear, which is the only reason that is tolerable.
  * THE MOTOR'S EJECTION CHARGE MASS. Assumed, not obtained. The conclusion does not
    turn on it -- see the table above -- but the number should be replaced.
  * MOUNT_TUBE_WALL_OPTIONS is the band the common 54 mm offerings fall in, not a
    quoted catalogue. Confirm the vendor's own list before ordering.
