  THE FLOOR IS GRAVITY DROOP DURING BOOST, and it is worth about 20 degrees.
  While the motor burns, the velocity vector rotates down at g cos(gamma)/V,
  and V starts at 21 m/s off the rail. Integrating over the 2.8 s burn:
      delta_gamma ~ (g/a) ln(V_bo/V_rail) = (9.81/64) ln(160/21) = 19.9 deg
  So a 20 deg rail is DESCENDING by burnout and hits the ground before the
  control law is allowed to start. A truly horizontal launch is not available
  on a T/W of 6; 25 deg is the practical floor and it flies flat (fpa ~6 deg
  at burnout) because the boost has already spent the elevation.
