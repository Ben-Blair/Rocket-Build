2. IMU SUPPLY NOISE -- old (shared +3V3) vs new (own LP2985 from VIN), ngspice
============================================================================================

  LP2985 legacy silicon: 45 dB at 1 kHz   |   buck in power-save at 1.12 MHz, 345 mA peaks
                                        OLD: IMU on +3V3     NEW: +3V3_IMU   better by
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  servo frame + MCU bursts (<50 kHz)          7.047 mVpp        1.610 mVpp     12.8 dB
  regulator switching (MHz)                   0.312 mVpp        0.020 mVpp     23.7 dB
  total rms (+ LDO self-noise)                 2711 uVrms         293 uVrms     19.3 dB
  (for scale: VIN itself swings 148 mVpp with the servos working; +3V3 at the buck 3.6 mVpp switching ripple)

  LP2985 new silicon: 70 dB at 1 kHz   |   buck in power-save at 1.12 MHz, 345 mA peaks
                                        OLD: IMU on +3V3     NEW: +3V3_IMU   better by
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  servo frame + MCU bursts (<50 kHz)          7.047 mVpp        0.039 mVpp     45.2 dB
  regulator switching (MHz)                   0.312 mVpp        0.001 mVpp     48.5 dB
  total rms (+ LDO self-noise)                 2711 uVrms          34 uVrms     38.0 dB
  (for scale: VIN itself swings 148 mVpp with the servos working; +3V3 at the buck 3.6 mVpp switching ripple)

  The ICM-42688-P datasheet gives no supply-rejection figure, so this is reported as
  volts at the pin, not dps.  What it establishes is the RATIO: the IMU's supply is
  now isolated from both the servo current on the pack and every load on +3V3.

3. MAGNETOMETER -- field from the board's own VBATT copper + plane return at stall
============================================================================================
  sensor at (162.0, 110.5) mm, 58 mm from the pack pads
  supply copper alone             24.809 mgauss
  IF the return used the planes   25.288 mgauss   (the board before the star ground)
  AS BUILT (SERVO_GND star return)   3.221 mgauss   against the 18.0 mgauss allowance
  compare: ONE untwisted 1 A servo lead 50 mm away = 40 mgauss (docs/14: twist them).
  VERDICT: board copper uses 17.9 % of the magnetometer allowance at the 4.1 A worst
  case -- NOT negligible.  The servo leads, off-board, remain the real risk.
  DC is the worst case for a PLANE return (it spreads by resistance); the star return
  is a trace, so it stays beside/under the trunk at every frequency.
