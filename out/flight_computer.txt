THE GYRO QUESTION docs/06 LEFT OPEN -- closed, by dropping the requirement
============================================================================================
  selected part           ICM-42688-P, +/-2000 dps
  roll rate at the cap        566 deg/s   28.3% of full scale   (3.5x margin)
  roll rate uncapped         2602 deg/s  130.1% -- SATURATED, and this is a FAULT condition, not a commanded one
  required IMU rate          1000 Hz against 32000 Hz available
  accel full scale             16 g against a 6.3 g peak (2.5x)

  The +/-4000 dps option is REAL and is rejected on the numbers, not for want of a
  part -- see design/flight_computer.GYRO_ALTERNATE_ICM45686:
    ICM-45686 gyro (+/-4000 dps alternative)
    range 4000 dps, ARW 3.8 mdps/sqrt(Hz) against the 42688's 2.8
    Saturation is DETECTABLE and is wired to the R12 latch instead of bought around.

LOGGING -- 'onboard flash is enough' was never checked, and it is not
============================================================================================
  channel                                           B/sample       Hz        B/s
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  raw gyro + accel, 6 x int16 + timestamp                 16     1000      16000
  magnetometer, 3 x int16 + timestamp                     10      100       1000
  baro pressure + temperature, 2 x int32 + ts             12       50        600
  GNSS position, velocity, fix quality                    36       10        360
  estimator state: quaternion, roll, rate                 24       71       1695
  control: 4 servo commands, phase, flags                 12       71        847
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  total                                                                    20502

  window logged           239 s (pad reference + boost + coast + descent)
      that window is a deliberate UPPER BOUND -- the whole descent is priced at the main's 5 m/s
      when the drogue leg is much faster. Against the requirement's own 150 s window it is 3.08 MB, still 3.9x short. The conclusion does not depend on which window is used.
  per flight              4.90 MB
  STM32F405 flash         1.05 MB total, 0.79 MB after a 256 KB firmware allowance
      margin              0.16x -- 6.2x SHORT
  W25Q128JVSIQ            17 MB, margin 3.4x, 3.4 flights

  The IMU line is 78% of the rate and it is the one that cannot be cut: logging raw
  gyro and accel at the PROPAGATION rate is what turns GV-2 into a measurement of
  Cm_delta and Cl_delta. At the loop rate it would fit on-chip and the flight the
  thesis rests on would stop producing the data it exists to produce.

============================================================================================
BUS MAP -- SPI for everything flight-critical, no I2C
============================================================================================
  SPI1  (up to 24 MHz)   CS1  ICM-42688-P      burst read, DMA, 1 kHz+
        dedicated to the IMU so its transfer never queues behind anything
  SPI2                   CS2  MMC5983MA        magnetometer, 100 Hz
                         CS3  MS5611           barometer, 50 Hz
  SPI3                   CS4  W25Q128JV        log flash, write-mostly
  USART1                      MAX-M10S         GNSS, 10 Hz, UBX binary
  USART2                      debug / console  bring-up only
  TIM4 CH1-4             PB6 PB7 PB8 PB9       4x servo PWM at 333 Hz (1520 us)
  SWD                         PA13 PA14        bring-up and flashing

  KST X08 Plus signal is 3.3-5.0 V HIGH, so the F405's 3.3 V timer outputs drive it
  directly with no level shift. Its 1520 us / 333 Hz frame is the datasheet's own,
  which is where avionics.board_requirements()'s 333 Hz figure independently lands.
