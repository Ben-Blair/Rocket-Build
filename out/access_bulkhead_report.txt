
============================================================================================
WHY THESE TWO WERE NEVER SIZED
============================================================================================

  design/joints.py puts a bulkhead on all four tube-to-tube joints. Two are
  SEPARATION joints and design/seal.py sizes both. The other two are ACCESS joints
  -- come apart by hand or by screws, not on an ejection charge -- and seal.py has
  carried a note since it was written that it does not size either of them. Both
  already spend bay length in joints.budgets() as a 12 mm allowance, so they were
  priced while not existing as parts. Neither is in the CAD, which is why the
  forward wiring pass-through has never been drawn -- the plate it passes through
  was not there.

============================================================================================
THE HEADLINE: NEITHER PLATE IS SIZED BY STRESS
============================================================================================

                                     pass-through plate    nose aft face

  thickness, mm                                     2.4              3.2
  mass, g                                          19.5             26.0
  governing pressure, Pa                            5.5          14943.3
  plate margin                                  302112x             199x
  feed-through margin                           162150x             107x
  point-load margin                                 n/a             121x
  assembled stack, mm                               5.4              6.2

  Both clear the thinnest stocked G-10 sheet by more than 25x on every load this
  file can find. That is the same shape of result design/venting.py reported for
  the module vents: the argument everyone reaches for -- 'this is a bulkhead, size
  it like one' -- is not what sizes these two. What sets them is producibility:
  holding a screw thread through repeated disassembly, and a connector's panel-nut
  torque. Neither has a stress model in this project, so a practical floor stands
  in for one, stated rather than hidden inside a 'looks about right' thickness.

============================================================================================
THE PASS-THROUGH PLATE -- nav bay / canard module
============================================================================================

  Both bays it separates vent to ambient ON THEIR OWN: the nav bay through its
  static ports, the canard module through its own wall (correction 28). Neither is
  sealed, so the only load that can develop across THIS plate is the difference in
  how fast the two bays track a changing ambient pressure -- not either bay's own
  lag, which venting.py already showed does not bind by a factor of 300.

  nav bay lag at 177 m/s          0.15 Pa
  canard module lag               5.69 Pa    (smaller vent area, so it lags MORE)
  differential across plate       5.54 Pa    24.34 mN total

  The ejection charge does not reach this plate either. It fires in the recovery
  bay's forward compartment against the AFT gas seal, and a leak past that disc is
  deliberately routed to the canard module's OWN vents -- venting.MODULE_VENT_STATION
  sits in the aft band specifically so a leak finds a hole before it travels forward
  to here.

  wire route (potted solid)   1 x dia 8.0 mm at R 22.6 mm -- NO WIRE GAUGE HAS EVER BEEN CHOSEN in this
                               project; this is a practical bundle allowance for 4
                               servo leads + the shared power/ground pair, flagged
                               the way seal.py flags JOINT_FRICTION_ALLOWANCE

============================================================================================
THE NOSE AFT FACE -- the module interface and the payload mount
============================================================================================

  Two loads, neither of them the aft gas seal's kind:

  1. TRAPPED DIFFERENTIAL, pad to apogee      14.94 kPa
     CONSERVATIVE, and said plainly rather than assumed away: nobody has ever
     modelled whether the nose cavity is vented. design/avionics.py and
     design/venting.py both stop at the nav bay. If the nose needs its own vent,
     that is a real open item this file surfaces rather than closes -- until it
     exists, the plate is checked as though the cavity is fully sealed.

  2. THE PAYLOAD MOUNT   300 g at 8.1 g boost accel = 23.8 N
     seal.py: this disc 'is what a future payload of up to about 300 g would hang
     from.' A tapered nose cone has nowhere else flat enough to cantilever a sled
     from, so that sentence is taken as the design intent -- a point load on a
     small boss, sized against the CORRECTION-32 PROVISION CEILING, not the 105 g
     of avionics actually flying today. Sizing the mount for what is built rather
     than what the vehicle is designed to carry is the harness's old mistake, one
     part further forward.

  the module's one electrical interface   1 x dia 8.0 mm at R 22.6 mm, panel-mount connector -- no part selected
